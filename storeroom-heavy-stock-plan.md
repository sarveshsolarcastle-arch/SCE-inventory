# Storeroom Storage Plan — Heavy & Humidity-Sensitive Stock

**Date finalized:** 2026-08-19
**Problem:** Standard shelving in the storeroom cannot safely hold heavy items (inverters, ACDBs, DCDBs, meters). Storeroom also runs at sustained 70-95% RH year-round, risking corrosion/damage to meters and electrical contacts. No dehumidifiers or RH sensors will be used (rejected as options). Floor storage was considered but rejected as not a professional/permanent solution.

## Final Decision

Two-zone shelving setup:

1. **Shelf 1 — Lightweight/general stock**: existing standard shelving, no changes needed.
2. **Shelf 2 — One dedicated heavy-duty rack** for heavy/expensive items (inverters, ACDB, DCDB, meters).

No dehumidifier, no RH sensors, no floor storage. Protection is handled passively, per-item, via sealed containers.

## Heavy-Duty Shelf Spec

- **Type**: Heavy-duty boltless rivet shelving (industrial slotted-angle style), 2-3 tiers.
- **Load rating**: 300 kg per tier minimum (covers inverters up to ~20 kg each plus panels comfortably).
- **Tier spacing**: Sized to tallest item + clearance; no cramming.
- **Deck material**: Solid steel or thick plywood (not wire mesh — flexes under point loads from boxy panels).
- **Anchoring**: Bolted to wall/floor — unanchored heavy racks with concentrated loads are a tip-over risk.
- **Bottom tier clearance**: 15-20 cm off the floor, for airflow and to avoid ground moisture/flood risk.
- **No stacking**: Every item gets its own slot on a tier — items are never stacked on top of each other (own weight would damage them).

### Tier Layout
- **Bottom tier**: heaviest inverters (lowest center of gravity = most stable).
- **Middle tier**: ACDB / DCDB panels.
- **Top tier**: meters, in sealed cases (lighter, easier access without heavy lifting).

## Item-Specific Humidity Protection (passive, no power/monitoring required)

### Meters (highest priority — most fragile, calibration-sensitive)
- Individual sealed IP65/IP67-rated airtight cases — one meter (or small batch) per case, not one shared bulk box.
- **VCI (vapor corrosion inhibitor) capsules/paper** as primary protection — passive, doesn't need monitoring or regeneration like silica gel does.
- Silica gel packets as a secondary/backup layer only, not primary defense at this RH range.
- Optional extra layer: heat-sealed foil/mylar moisture-barrier bags inside the case for long-term storage.

### ACDB / DCDB (panels with exposed bus bars/terminals)
- Check existing enclosure IP rating first — many already ship IP54-IP65; if so, just plug/seal cable glands and knockouts.
- If not sealed: fitted vinyl/PVC dust-and-damp covers.
- VCI strip/capsule placed inside near bus bar/terminal area.

### Inverters
- Cannot be fully airtight (ventilated by design) — use heavy-duty PVC/vinyl dust covers instead.
- VCI pouch placed near the ventilation intake, under the cover but not sealed shut.
- Reuse manufacturer packaging/crates where available — usually designed for this exposure risk already.

## Rejected Options (for reference)
- Dehumidifiers (room-level) — rejected.
- RH sensors/hygrometers for monitoring — rejected.
- Long-term floor storage — rejected as not a professional/permanent solution; one heavy shelf preferred instead.
- Silica gel as primary protection — rejected as insufficient at 70-95% RH without monitoring/regeneration capability; VCI preferred.
